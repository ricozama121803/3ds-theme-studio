9 Build & install

    Download .zip

    Applying it on your 3DS
    1. Put the SD card in your computer and click Save to SD card. Pick the SD card’s main folder and the theme goes into /Themes/. Or download the zip and copy it into /Themes/ yourself.
    2. Put the card back in the 3DS and open Anemone3DS (requires custom firmware).
    3. Select your theme and apply it with BGM if it has music.
    4. On the Home Menu, open Theme Settings and make sure theme shuffle is off.
